Student Registration

How it works

  1. Students register their email, mobile number, and municipality.
  2. An authorized project administrator reviews weather and class-suspension information.
  3. The administrator sends an update to registered students in the selected municipality.

Important: This prototype does not automatically read PAGASA advisories or decide whether classes are suspended. PAGASA issues weather information; suspension decisions follow applicable government rules and local official announcements. Always verify with your LGU and school.
